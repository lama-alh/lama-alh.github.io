An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing the Consumer Price Index across 16 Saudi cities (2019–2025), answering one question: did prices rise equally everywhere? It shows what 100 SAR from 2019 is worth today per city, a city ranking, a map, and annual inflation against the national average.
